# AI-Generated Affiliate Account

## How to Create UGC Product Videos Using ChatGPT + Gemini 🎬

### STEP 1 — Upload Your Product Photo

Open ChatGPT and upload a clear image of your product. It can be clothing, skincare, cosmetics, gadgets, accessories, home products, or any other physical product.

### STEP 2 — Create a UGC Storyboard

Ask ChatGPT to create a 3×3 storyboard based on your uploaded product photo.

**Copy this prompt:**

```text
Create a photorealistic 3×3 UGC video storyboard for my uploaded product. Show nine different shots, including an opening hook, product close-ups, a realistic product demonstration, lifestyle shots, and a final hero shot. Keep the product's design, colour, proportions, and details consistent across all nine panels. Use realistic lighting and natural movements. No text, captions, or added logos.
```

Save the generated storyboard image.

### STEP 3 — Upload Both Images to Gemini

Open Google Gemini and select a video-generation model.

**Upload:**

1. Your original product photo.
2. Your generated 3×3 UGC storyboard.

The original photo helps establish product accuracy, while the storyboard guides the shot sequence and camera angles.

### STEP 4 — Copy the Universal Video Prompt

Paste the following prompt into Gemini along with both images.

#### UNIVERSAL 10-SECOND UGC VIDEO PROMPT

```text
Create a 10-second photorealistic UGC product video with audio, using the uploaded original product photo and 3×3 storyboard as visual references.

IMPORTANT:
The storyboard is a reference for camera angles, shot composition, and sequence. Do not animate the nine panels as a collage. Generate one full-screen vertical video with individual shots and smooth transitions.

PRODUCT CONSISTENCY

Preserve the exact product shown in the original reference image, including its identity, colour, shape, proportions, material appearance, packaging, labels, logos, and visible details. Keep the product consistent throughout the video.

If a person appears in the reference, maintain their identity and appearance across shots. Preserve the relevant background, lighting, and overall aesthetic.

Choose natural product interactions appropriate to the item, such as holding, wearing, applying, opening, operating, or demonstrating it. Only show actions that make sense for the product. Never invent features, functions, or benefits.

10-SECOND SHOT SEQUENCE

0–2 seconds — The Hook:
Open with an attractive, clear view of the product. Show the creator presenting it, revealing it, or demonstrating its most distinctive visible feature. Capture attention immediately.

2–4 seconds — Product Close-up:
Move closer to show the product's most important visual details, such as texture, design, packaging, finish, or craftsmanship. Keep the product sharp and accurate.

4–6 seconds — Product Demonstration:
Show one simple, realistic interaction that demonstrates how the product is used. Ensure natural movement, correct hand placement, and physically plausible actions.

6–8 seconds — Lifestyle Shot:
Present the product in a relevant setting or use situation. Keep it as the main focus and use subtle camera movement.

8–10 seconds — Final Hero Shot:
End with a clear, attractive presentation of the complete product or its finished result. Hold the final composition briefly so viewers can see the product properly.

Adapt all shots to the specific product. Do not force clothing-style shots onto gadgets, cosmetics, food, or other product categories.

VOICEOVER AND AUDIO

Generate a clear, natural-sounding adult voiceover in conversational English. Use a friendly, authentic UGC creator tone.

Use this adaptable dialogue, replacing the brackets only with accurate product information:

"Take a look at this [PRODUCT NAME]! The [VISIBLE FEATURE] really stands out, making it a great option for [VERIFIED USE OR BENEFIT]."

If the product details are unknown, say:

"Take a closer look at this product. Here are the details that make it stand out."

Begin speaking at approximately 0.3 seconds. Finish the dialogue naturally before the video ends. Add subtle background music at a low volume beneath the voiceover.

Keep the speech clear, correctly pronounced, and intelligible. Avoid overlapping voices, robotic speech, excessive sound effects, and unsupported product claims. Use voiceover rather than forced lip-sync unless the creator is visibly speaking.

VISUAL QUALITY

Use vertical 9:16 framing, ideally 1080 × 1920 where supported. Create realistic lighting, natural shadows, accurate colours, detailed product textures, smooth camera movements, consistent backgrounds, and believable interactions.

The result should resemble authentic, well-produced smartphone UGC content rather than an exaggerated cinematic advertisement.

STRICT REQUIREMENTS

- Exactly 10 seconds.
- One full-screen vertical video, not a collage or slideshow.
- No split screens or panel borders.
- No unnecessary sparkles or floating particles.
- No distorted hands or duplicated objects.
- No changing product designs or flickering details.
- No warped packaging or unnatural movements.
- No unrequested text, captions, or watermarks.
- No invented product features.

Preserve the reference product's appearance across every shot. Prioritise product accuracy and visual continuity over complicated camera movements.

Generate the actual video with audible speech and background music if supported by the selected video-generation model—not a written storyboard or another prompt.
```

### STEP 5 — Generate Your Video

Submit the prompt and generate your 10-second video.

Native voiceover and music depend on the Gemini video-generation model you select.

### STEP 6 — Review and Refine

Check the generated video for:

- Product changes.
- Incorrect logos.
- Distorted details.
- Unnatural movements.
- Abrupt transitions.
- Audio problems.

If something looks wrong, simplify the shot sequence and regenerate. Using fewer camera changes often helps maintain product consistency.

---

## 🎉 You're Done!

You can reuse this workflow for different products by uploading a new product image and storyboard each time.
