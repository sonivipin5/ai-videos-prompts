# AI Outfit Collage & Video Generation Prompts

## 1. Image Generation Prompt 🖼️

**IMAGE PROMPT:**

```text
Create a realistic 9:16 vertical collage with 4 panels using my uploaded outfit image:

Panel 1: Outfit packed in transparent plastic.

Panel 2: Top held by hands.

Panel 3: Bottom wear held by hands.

Panel 4: A young woman wearing the exact outfit in a full-length mirror selfie with her phone covering her face.

PRESERVE OUTFIT DETAILS:
Preserve the original clothing color, print, design, pattern, fabric texture, and fit exactly as shown in the uploaded reference image.

ENVIRONMENT & STYLE:
- Cozy bedroom setting.
- Warm, natural lighting.
- Realistic fabric texture.
- Photorealistic quality.
- Clean white panel borders.
- Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio.
- No text.
- No watermark.

Make all four panels visually consistent and realistic.
```

## 2. Video Generation Prompt 🎬

**VIDEO PROMPT:**

```text
Use this image as the visual reference.

Start the video from Panel 1 and smoothly progress through Panels 1 → 2 → 3 → 4.

Animate this 4-panel clothing collage realistically. Preserve the exact outfit, colors, print, fabric, and design.

PANEL-BY-PANEL ANIMATION:

Panel 1:
A hand naturally holds the packaged outfit with subtle plastic movement.

Panel 2:
Hands gently hold and adjust the top, showing its fabric and design details.

Panel 3:
Hands gently display the jeans/bottom wear, highlighting the fabric and overall design.

Panel 4:
The model wears the exact outfit and performs a subtle mirror-selfie pose. The phone must fully hide her face. The outfit must match the uploaded reference image exactly.

VISUAL QUALITY:
- Photorealistic movement.
- Realistic hands and fabric physics.
- Smooth transitions between panels.
- Consistent lighting and environment.
- Natural human movements.
- Maintain exact outfit consistency.

STRICT REQUIREMENTS:
- Never change the outfit.
- No clothing morphing.
- No face reveal.
- No distorted hands.
- No extra fingers.
- No text or watermark.
- Never show all four panels at once.
- Do not display the collage as a static four-panel layout throughout the video.

IMPORTANT:
Show each panel as an individual full-screen scene in sequence:
Panel 1 → Panel 2 → Panel 3 → Panel 4.

Use smooth cinematic transitions while maintaining the original outfit's appearance throughout the video.
```

**Note:** Always upload the original outfit image as a reference to maintain clothing accuracy during image and video generation.
